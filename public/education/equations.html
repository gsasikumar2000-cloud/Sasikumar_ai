🪙 Gold Calculations

⚖️ Weight

Value = Weight × Rate per gram
10g = 10 × 1g rate
8g = 8 × 1g rate

Always confirm the applicable market rate before calculating a transaction.

🔶 Purity

Pure Gold = Weight × Purity ÷ 100
22K Purity ≈ 91.6%
18K Purity = 75%
24K Purity ≈ 99.9%

🔢 Karat Conversion

Purity% = Karat ÷ 24 × 100
Karat = Purity% × 24 ÷ 100

💰 Gold Value

Gold Value = Net Weight × Applicable Rate
Net Weight = Gross Weight − Stone/Other Weight

💎 Making Charges

Making Charge = Gold Value × Making% ÷ 100
Subtotal = Gold Value + Making Charge

🧾 General Bill

Subtotal = Gold + Making + Other Charges
Final Amount = Subtotal + Applicable Tax

Tax and charges depend on the applicable rules and transaction.

💰 Finance Maths

📈 Simple Interest

SI = (P × R × T) ÷ 100
Amount = P + SI

📊 Compound Interest

A = P(1 + R/100)ⁿ
CI = A − P

🏦 EMI

EMI = P × r × (1+r)ⁿ ÷ ((1+r)ⁿ−1)

P = Principal, r = monthly interest rate, n = number of monthly instalments.

💵 Loan Interest

Interest = Amount Paid − Principal
Total Payment = EMI × Number of EMIs

🏦 FD

Interest depends on Principal, Rate, Time and compounding method
Maturity = Principal + Interest

💳 RD

Total Deposit = Monthly Deposit × Number of Months
Maturity = Deposits + Applicable Interest
