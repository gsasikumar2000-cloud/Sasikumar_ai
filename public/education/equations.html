📚 SASIKUMAR AI – Gold Equations

⚖️ Weight

Weight × Rate = Approximate Gold Value

🪙 Purity

Fine Gold = Gross Weight × Purity ÷ 100

💰 Gold Value

Gold Value = Fine Gold Weight × Applicable Gold Rate

🏦 Loan Calculation

Indicative Loan = Eligible Gold Value × Applicable LTV

🔬 Density

Density = Mass ÷ Volume

Note: These equations are for educational and estimation purposes. Actual appraisal, assay and lending decisions may require professional testing and the applicable institution's rules.
