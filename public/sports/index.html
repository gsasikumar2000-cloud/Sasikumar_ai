Basics: Two teams attempt to score by putting the ball into the opponent's goal.

History: Invented by James Naismith in 1891 in the United States.
Basics: Teams attack a basket and defend their own basket.

History: Modern lawn tennis developed in Britain during the 19th century.
Basics: Singles or doubles, serving and returning over a net.

History: Modern badminton developed from earlier racket-and-shuttle games.
Basics: Players hit a shuttlecock over a net.

❓ Sports Knowledge

Why are sports rules important?

Rules create a common structure for competition, define scoring and help officials manage matches fairly and consistently.

What is a tournament?

A tournament is an organized competition involving multiple players or teams, usually with a defined schedule and competition format.

What is a live score?

A live score is a real-time or near-real-time update of the score and match status while a competition is taking place.
