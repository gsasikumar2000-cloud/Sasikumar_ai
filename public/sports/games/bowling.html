📚 Bowling Knowledge

• Ten-pin bowling uses ten pins arranged in a triangle.

• A strike means all pins are knocked down with the first roll of a frame.

• A spare means all pins are knocked down using two rolls in a frame.

• Accuracy and controlled ball speed are useful bowling skills.
